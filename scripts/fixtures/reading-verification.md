## The Three Card Locator

Creator: Mira Sol (a fictional author created only for this test).

Effect: A spectator remembers one of three face-down cards. The performer locates it after one simple exchange.

Props: Three ordinary cards and a table. Do not use a coin, wallet, glue, flame, or duplicate card.

Preparation: Secretly remember the bottom card of the three-card packet. This is the key card.

Procedure:
1. Spread the three cards and have the spectator remember the top card without changing the packet order.
2. Ask the spectator to place that selected card on the table.
3. Place the remaining two-card packet on top of the selected card. The remembered key card is now directly above the selection.
4. Turn the packet face up, spread it, locate the key card, and reveal the card directly beneath it in the packet order.

Practice: Repeat the sequence five times with the same three cards. Verify that the key card stays immediately above the selection every time.
